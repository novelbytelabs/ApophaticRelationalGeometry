# Minimal Three-Node Model

The first executable model uses three scalar node states on a complete undirected graph.

## Collective mode

The whole-state observable is

$$
c(x)=\frac{1}{3}\sum_{i=1}^{3}x_i^2.
$$

It is generated by the local states and acts back on every local state.

## Local state dynamics

For node $i$,

$$
\dot x_i
=
\alpha x_i
-
\beta x_i^3
+
\sum_{j\neq i}w_{ij}(x_j-x_i)
-
\chi c(x)x_i.
$$

The conductance is

$$
w_{ij}=a_{ij}\exp(-q_{ij}),
$$

where

$$
a_{ij}=\sigma(s_{ij}).
$$

## Edge activation dynamics

$$
\tau_s\dot s_{ij}
=
\eta_0
-
\eta_1(x_i-x_j)^2
+
\eta_2c(x)
-
s_{ij}.
$$

## Metric deformation dynamics

$$
\tau_q\dot q_{ij}
=
-\gamma q_{ij}
+
\kappa(x_i-x_j)^2
-
\rho c(x).
$$

## Intrinsic edge length

$$
\ell_{ij}
=
\frac{\exp(q_{ij})}{\sigma(s_{ij})}
\sqrt{\epsilon^2+(x_i-x_j)^2}.
$$

The graph metric is the shortest-path distance generated by these edge lengths.

## Interpretation

- Local nonlinear terms provide individual dynamics.
- Neighbor differences provide local coupling.
- The collective mode represents a whole generated by the many.
- Edge activation and metric deformation make connectivity and geometry state-dependent.
- The model is a prototype, not a claim that these exact equations are physical law.
