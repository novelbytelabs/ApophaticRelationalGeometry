# Original Minimal Three-Node Model: $M_F$

This document records the original executable collective-feedback prototype. It is now one member of the frozen four-model family. The matched no-feedback baseline $M_0$ is implemented separately in `src/apophatic_geometry/models.py`.

The model described here is $M_F$, not projected geometry.

## State

The model uses three scalar node states on a complete undirected graph, with edge activation variables $s_{ij}$ and metric deformation variables $q_{ij}$:

$$
Z_F=(x,s,q).
$$

The collective statistic is algebraically derived from $x$ rather than stored as an independent state variable.

## Collective mode

$$
c(x)=\frac{1}{3}\sum_{i=1}^{3}x_i^2.
$$

It is generated by the node states and feeds back into node, edge, and metric transitions.

The explicit substrate path is

$$
x\longrightarrow c(x)\longrightarrow(\dot x,\dot s,\dot q).
$$

## Local state dynamics

For node $i$,

$$
\dot x_i
=
\alpha x_i
-
\beta x_i^3
+
\sum_{j\neq i}w_{ij}(x_j-x_i)
-
\chi c(x)x_i.
$$

The conductance is

$$
w_{ij}=a_{ij}\exp(-q_{ij}),
$$

where

$$
a_{ij}=\sigma(s_{ij}).
$$

## Edge activation dynamics

$$
\tau_s\dot s_{ij}
=
\eta_0
-
\eta_1(x_i-x_j)^2
+
\eta_2c(x)
-
s_{ij}.
$$

## Metric deformation dynamics

$$
\tau_q\dot q_{ij}
=
-\gamma q_{ij}
+
\kappa(x_i-x_j)^2
-
\rho c(x).
$$

## Intrinsic edge length

$$
\ell_{ij}
=
\frac{\exp(q_{ij})}{\sigma(s_{ij})}
\sqrt{\epsilon^2+(x_i-x_j)^2}.
$$

The graph metric is the shortest-path distance generated by these edge lengths.

## Implemented claim

The model supports only:

> **implemented prototype-level downward feedback/constraint**

It does not establish macro-level causal autonomy, organization dependence beyond the encoded statistic, transport, counterfactual adequacy, alternative-model defeat, or empirical strong emergence.

## Relationship to $M_0$

$M_0$ removes the three feedback coefficients:

$$
\chi=\eta_2=\rho=0.
$$

The implementation unit-tests the exact frozen-equation reduction

$$
M_F(\chi=\eta_2=\rho=0)=M_0.
$$

Both $M_0$ and $M_F$ are now implemented and unit-tested.

## Projection status

This model defines no $\Gamma$, $H$, tangent space, or projection operator. It therefore does not implement $M_P$ or $M_{FP}$.

Current ceiling:

$$
\boxed{
M_0,M_F\ \text{implemented and unit-tested};
\quad
M_P,\ M_{FP},\ \text{and }M_F\equiv M_P\ \text{unverified}.
}
$$

## Interpretation

- Local nonlinear terms provide individual dynamics.
- Neighbor differences provide local coupling.
- The collective statistic provides endogenous feedback.
- Edge activation and metric deformation make connectivity and geometry state dependent.
- The equations are a prototype, not a physical law.
