# Original Minimal Three-Node Model: $M_F$

This document records the original executable collective-feedback prototype. It is one member of the frozen four-model family.

The model described here is $M_F$, not projected geometry.

## State

The model uses three scalar node states on a complete undirected graph, with edge activation variables $s_{ij}$ and metric deformation variables $q_{ij}$:

$$
Z_F=(x,s,q).
$$

The collective statistic is algebraically derived from $x.

## Collective mode

$$
c(x)=\frac13\sum_{i=1}^{3}x_i^2.
$$

It feeds back into node, edge, and metric transitions through

$$
x\longrightarrow c(x)\longrightarrow(\dot x,\dot s,\dot q).
$$

## Node dynamics

$$
\dot x_i
=
\alpha x_i-
\beta x_i^3+
\sum_{j\neq i}w_{ij}(x_j-x_i)-
\chi c(x)x_i,
$$

where

$$
w_{ij}=\sigma(s_{ij})e^{-q_{ij}}.
$$

## Edge activation

$$
\tau_s\dot s_{ij}
=
\eta_0-
\eta_1(x_i-x_j)^2+
\eta_2c(x)-s_{ij}.
$$

## Metric deformation

$$
\tau_q\dot q_{ij}
=
-\gamma q_{ij}+
\kappa(x_i-x_j)^2-
\rho c(x).
$$

## Intrinsic edge length

$$
\ell_{ij}
=
\frac{e^{q_{ij}}}{\sigma(s_{ij})}
\sqrt{\epsilon^2+(x_i-x_j)^2}.
$$

The graph metric is the shortest-path distance generated by these positive edge lengths.

## Implemented claim

The model supports only:

> **implemented prototype-level downward feedback/constraint**

It does not establish macro-level causal autonomy, organization dependence beyond the encoded statistic, transport, counterfactual adequacy, alternative-model defeat, or empirical strong emergence.

## Relationship to $M_0$

$M_0$ removes the three feedback coefficients:

$$
\chi=\eta_2=\rho=0.
$$

The implementation unit-tests

$$
M_F(\chi=\eta_2=\rho=0)=M_0.
$$

## Relationship to $M_P$

$M_F$ defines no $\Gamma$, $H$, tangent space, or projection operator. It remains a feedback model even though $M_P$ is now implemented elsewhere.

$M_P$ uses the separate constant-amplitude constraint

$$
\Gamma(Z)=\frac13x^Tx-c_0=0
$$

and removes the normal component of the $M_0$ node proposal. Implementation of $M_P$ does not establish

$$
M_F\equiv M_P.
$$

## Current ceiling

$$
\boxed{
M_0,M_F,M_P\ \text{implemented and unit-tested};
\quad
M_{FP}\ \text{and }M_F\equiv M_P\ \text{unverified}.
}
$$

## Interpretation

- Local nonlinear terms provide individual dynamics.
- Neighbor differences provide local coupling.
- The collective statistic provides endogenous feedback.
- Edge activation and metric deformation make connectivity and geometry state dependent.
- The equations are a prototype, not a physical law.
