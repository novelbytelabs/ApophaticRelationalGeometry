## Closest-framework procedure

For each candidate neighboring framework:

1. reproduce its governing equations in the project notation;
2. map its variables into $Z=(x,s,q,\theta,c)$ where possible;
3. identify exact correspondences;
4. identify terms with no correspondence;
5. test whether those terms can be removed by reparameterization;
6. compare observable predictions under matched initial conditions;
7. record whether the full model is broader, equivalent, or merely differently interpreted.

## Novelty threat levels

- **Level 0 — No threat:** only superficial terminology overlaps.
- **Level 1 — Ingredient overlap:** one component is already standard.
- **Level 2 — Structural overlap:** several coupled components already appear together.
- **Level 3 — Near equivalence:** a known framework maps into the proposed system with minor changes.
- **Level 4 — Full equivalence:** the proposed mechanism is a reparameterization or reinterpretation of existing work.

A Level 4 result requires withdrawal of the mathematical novelty claim, but may leave a valid synthesis, implementation, or interpretive contribution.
