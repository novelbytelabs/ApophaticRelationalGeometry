# Scope and Status

## Research question

Can a system be modeled as one globally coherent process and many locally real processes at the same time, without treating the whole as an external controller or the parts as independently fundamental?

## Working hypothesis

A candidate geometry can be built from:

$$
\text{local nonlinear proposals}
+
\text{dynamic relations}
+
\text{state-dependent metric}
+
\text{global admissibility constraints}.
$$

The whole is represented operationally by compatibility conditions on the complete configuration. The many are represented by local states, local rules, and neighborhood interactions.

## What is claimed

- The formalism is mathematically definable.
- It can be implemented and falsified.
- It distinguishes local proposed motion from globally admissible motion.
- Its intrinsic geometry changes with state and relation strength.

## What is not claimed

- That this is the final ontology.
- That the universe literally is a graph.
- That the apophatic principle proves any religion or metaphysics.
- That primes, emptiness, computation, or coherence are established as ultimate substances.
- That the prototype presently reproduces known physical laws.

## Standard of progress

Progress requires proofs, executable definitions, ablations, independent references, and predictions that differ from simpler models.
