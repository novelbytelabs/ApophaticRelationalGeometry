# Apophatic Non-Reification Schema

Let $T$ be a formal theory, $\mathfrak M$ a model of $T$, and $O$ an object interpreted within $\mathfrak M$.

The schema states:

$$
\boxed{
\mathfrak M\models\varphi(O)
\quad\not\Longrightarrow\quad
\operatorname{Absolute}(O).
}
$$

Here $\operatorname{Absolute}$ is metalinguistic. It is not an internal mathematical predicate.

In plain language:

> No theorem establishing the formal role of an object licenses identifying that object with context-independent reality.

This preserves ordinary mathematics. Sets, graphs, manifolds, kernels, categories, constraints, and invariants remain valid constructions. The schema rejects only the inference

$$
\text{successful representation}
\quad\Longrightarrow\quad
\text{ontological identity}.
$$

## Presentation invariance

Let $\mathcal P$ be a family of presentations and $\mathcal G$ a groupoid of admissible transformations. A structural claim $Q$ must satisfy

$$
Q(P)=Q(P')
$$

whenever an admissible map $g:P\to P'$ exists.

Even then,

$$
Q\in\operatorname{Inv}_{\mathcal G}(\mathcal P)
$$

means invariance relative to $\mathcal G$, not invariance under every conceivable context.

## Self-application

The schema is not declared the final ontology. It is a discipline governing interpretation under the current research program.
