# Phase 5 Comparative Experiment Protocol v1.0

## Status

**Frozen before trajectory generation.**

This protocol defines the first falsification-oriented comparison of

$$
M_0,\qquad M_F,\qquad M_P,\qquad M_{FP}.
$$

At freeze time, all four contract-v1.0 prototypes are implemented and software-verified. No Phase 5 trajectory, pilot result, or confirmatory result has been generated or inspected.

The maximum authorization after this gate is:

$$
\boxed{
\text{development pilot authorized};
\qquad
\text{confirmatory execution and scientific claims remain blocked}.
}
$$

## Binding claim ceiling

$$
\boxed{
M_0,M_F,M_P,M_{FP}\ \text{implemented and unit-tested};
\quad
M_F\equiv M_P\ \text{unverified}.
}
$$

The protocol tests numerical behavior of declared mechanisms. It cannot establish physical adequacy, strong emergence, macro-level causal autonomy, mathematical novelty, or ontological truth.

## 2. Primary hypothesis

### H1 — feedback and projection are dynamically distinguishable

The primary comparison is

$$
M_F\leftrightarrow M_P
$$

under the full-state observation map

$$
O_{\mathrm{full}}(Z)=(x,s,q)\in\mathbb R^9.
$$

For sampled trajectories \(A=(a_k)\) and \(B=(b_k)\), define

$$
D(A,B)
=
\sqrt{
\frac{
2\sum_k\|a_k-b_k\|_2^2
}{
\sum_k\|a_k\|_2^2+\sum_k\|b_k\|_2^2+10^{-30}
}
}.
$$

For each direction and amplitude configuration, compute

$$
D_{\mathrm{full}}(M_F,M_P).
$$

The five amplitude conditions belonging to one direction are repeated conditions, not independent samples. Their median is the direction-level effect. The primary estimator is the median across the fourteen held-out confirmatory directions.

### Positive outcome

Declare `DETECTED` only when:

1. the lower 95% percentile-bootstrap bound for the direction-level median exceeds

   $$
   \delta_{\mathrm{detect}}=0.02;
   $$

2. at least 80% of direction effects exceed \(0.02\); and
3. those detected effects are at least ten times their direction-specific numerical floors.

This is a 2% normalized trajectory-separation threshold chosen before execution. It has no universal physical meaning.

### Negative outcome

Declare `EQUIVALENT_WITHIN_PROTOCOL_MARGIN` only when:

1. the upper 95% bootstrap bound is below

   $$
   \delta_{\mathrm{eq}}=0.002;
   $$

2. every direction effect is below \(0.002\); and
3. every numerical floor is below \(\delta_{\mathrm{eq}}/3\).

This is scoped observational equivalence only. It is not mathematical identity.

### Inconclusive outcome

Every other valid result is `INCONCLUSIVE`. Numerical or integrity failures can instead invalidate the decision entirely.

## 3. Secondary hypotheses

### H2 — indirect \(M_{FP}\) node divergence

At the same regular full state,

$$
f_{FP}=f_P.
$$

The protocol tests whether retained \(s/q\) feedback nevertheless produces later node-trajectory divergence:

$$
D_x(M_P,M_{FP})>0.01.
$$

This is a developmental secondary outcome.

### H3 — feedback effect

Compare

$$
M_0\leftrightarrow M_F
$$

under \(O_{\mathrm{full}}\), with a developmental detection threshold of \(0.02\).

### H4 — projection effect

Compare

$$
M_0\leftrightarrow M_P
$$

under \(O_{\mathrm{full}}\), with a developmental detection threshold of \(0.02\).

### H5 — projected constraint gate

For every accepted \(M_P\) and \(M_{FP}\) step,

$$
|c(x)-c_0|
\le
10^{-12}\max(1,c_0)
$$

after retraction.

### H6 — same-state node identity gate

At every prespecified sampled regular state used for this diagnostic, evaluate both projected derivatives on the same state and require normalized node-derivative disagreement no larger than \(10^{-12}\).

H5 and H6 are mandatory validity gates, not evidence of scientific superiority.

## 4. Frozen initial-condition domain

The domain contains twenty-four deterministic directions on the \(z>0\) hemisphere, generated by a Fibonacci spherical design:

$$
z_k=\frac{k+1/2}{24},
$$

$$
\phi_k=k\pi(3-\sqrt5),
$$

$$
u_k=
\left(
\sqrt{1-z_k^2}\cos\phi_k,
\sqrt{1-z_k^2}\sin\phi_k,
z_k
\right),
\qquad
k=0,\ldots,23.
$$

The hemisphere avoids treating the global-sign symmetry \(x\mapsto-x\) as independent replication.

For every direction:

$$
c_0\in\{0.05,0.10,0.25,0.50,1.00\},
$$

$$
x_0=\sqrt{3c_0}\,u_k.
$$

All runs share

$$
s_0=(0.2,-0.1,0.1),
\qquad
q_0=(0,0.05,-0.05).
$$

This produces 120 configurations.

## 5. Pilot and confirmatory separation

The split is performed at the direction level. For each direction identifier, compute:

```text
SHA256(direction_id + "|ARG-P5-COMP-v1")
```

The ten lexicographically smallest hashes are pilot directions. The remaining fourteen are confirmatory directions.

Therefore:

- pilot: 10 directions, 50 configurations;
- confirmatory: 14 directions, 70 configurations.

The split is public, deterministic, and frozen before any trajectory generation. Confirmatory trajectories are forbidden during the development pilot.

## 7. Numerical policy

### Reference integration

Use fixed-step classical RK4 with:

$$
T=10,
\qquad
\Delta t=10^{-3}.
$$

Record 1001 observations at intervals of \(0.01\), including \(t=0\).

### Mandatory refinement

Every decision-bearing run is repeated at:

$$
10^{-3},
\qquad
5\times10^{-4},
\qquad
2.5\times10^{-4}.
$$

Require the discrepancy ordering

$$
D(\Delta t/2,\Delta t/4)
<
D(\Delta t,\Delta t/2)
$$

for every observation map used in a decision.

The endpoint must satisfy

$$
\|O_{\Delta t/2}(T)-O_{\Delta t/4}(T)\|_\infty
\le
10^{-6}
+
10^{-5}\|O_{\Delta t/4}(T)\|_\infty.
$$

### Alternate integrator

Use SciPy `solve_ivp` with `DOP853`:

```text
rtol = 1e-10
atol = 1e-12
max_step = 0.001
```

Projected models are integrated in \(0.001\)-length segments, with the same radial retraction applied at every segment boundary.

Require

$$
D(\mathrm{RK4}_{\Delta t/4},\mathrm{DOP853})
\le
\max\left(
10^{-5},
10D(\mathrm{RK4}_{\Delta t/2},\mathrm{RK4}_{\Delta t/4})
\right).
$$

Failure is retained and reported. It is not repaired by changing the solver after inspection.

## 8. Numerical floor

For each configuration and observation map, the numerical floor is the maximum of:

- the RK4 \(\Delta t/2\) versus \(\Delta t/4\) discrepancy;
- the RK4 \(\Delta t/4\) versus DOP853 discrepancy;

over both models in the comparison.

The direction-level floor is the maximum over its five amplitude conditions.

This floor is used in the primary ten-to-one effect-versus-numerics requirement.

## 9. Interventions and ablations

The canonical mechanism removals are already encoded by the four models:

- remove feedback: \(M_{FP}\to M_P\) and \(M_F\to M_0\);
- remove projection: \(M_{FP}\to M_F\) and \(M_P\to M_0\);
- remove both: \(M_{FP}\to M_0\).

Additional developmental controls are frozen:

- exogenous replay of the paired \(M_F\) signal \(c(t)\), with piecewise-linear interpolation and no fitting;
- hold \(s=s_0\);
- hold \(q=q_0\);
- hold both \(s=s_0\) and \(q=q_0\);
- all six node permutations as software tripwires.

A non-isomorphic graph intervention is excluded because contract v1.0 fixes the complete three-node graph. A graph-incidence change requires a new contract version. The soft-penalty control remains excluded.

## 10. Inferential units and uncertainty

The independent design unit is the direction identifier.

The following are not independent:

- the five \(c_0\) conditions within one direction;
- time points;
- solver refinements;
- alternate-integrator runs;
- node permutations.

The primary interval uses 50,000 percentile-bootstrap resamples of the direction units with fixed PCG64 seed `20260801`. It represents variability across the frozen design directions, not random sampling from a natural population.

Secondary effect intervals use a 98.75% level as a four-family Bonferroni descriptive safeguard. Secondary results cannot promote claims.

## 11. Failure and missing-run rules

No imputation is allowed.

A direction is complete only when all five amplitude conditions and both models in the relevant comparison pass every mandatory numerical gate.

The primary decision is blocked when:

- more than one confirmatory direction is incomplete;
- any H5 or H6 gate fails;
- any relabeling tripwire fails;
- any lock hash differs;
- configuration labels or splits are inconsistent.

All failed and singular runs remain in the archive.

## 12. Pilot stop rules

Stop immediately on:

- code-equation parity failure;
- protocol lock mismatch;
- split contamination;
- permutation-equivariance failure;
- undeclared fallback, clipping, or pseudoinverse behavior.

Pause and diagnose when more than 10% of pilot configurations fail numerical acceptance.

Thresholds, primary metrics, and the held-out split cannot be relaxed after effects are inspected.

## 14. Outcome meanings

### Positive

A positive H1 result means \(M_F\) and \(M_P\) are numerically distinguishable under this protocol and full-state observation map.

### Negative

A negative H1 result means only that they are observationally equivalent within the frozen \(0.002\) margin over this domain.

### Inconclusive

The effect falls between the margins, uncertainty crosses a boundary, or completion criteria are insufficient.

### Invalid

Integrity, invariant, solver, lock, or missing-run gates prevent evaluation.

None of these outcomes establishes a fundamental geometry or physical theory.
