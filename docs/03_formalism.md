# Dynamic Relational Constraint Geometry

## Status of this document

This document specifies the **target ARG model family**, including projected-admissibility variants. It is not a description of everything currently implemented.

Current executable status:

$$
M_F\ \text{implemented and unit-tested};
\qquad
M_P,\ M_{FP},\ M_F\equiv M_P\ \text{unverified}.
$$

The current three-node executable implements endogenous collective feedback, adaptive relations, and state-dependent intrinsic distance. It does not yet implement the $\Gamma/H$ projection defined below.

## 1. Provisional presentation

Let

$$
\bar G=(V,\bar E)
$$

be a potential undirected graph. A general target state may be written

$$
Z=(x,s,q,\theta,c).
$$

For node $i$ and edge $e$:

$$
x_i\in M_i,
\qquad
s_e,q_e\in\mathbb R.
$$

The active edge strength is

$$
a_e=\sigma(s_e)=\frac{1}{1+\exp(-s_e)}.
$$

## 2. Relational mismatch

For $e=(i,j)$, define endpoint maps into a shared comparison space $Y_e$:

$$
\rho_{ie}:M_i\times\Theta_i\times C\to Y_e,
$$

$$
\rho_{je}:M_j\times\Theta_j\times C\to Y_e.
$$

The mismatch is

$$
\delta_e(Z)=\rho_{ie}(x_i,\theta_i,c)-\rho_{je}(x_j,\theta_j,c).
$$

A coherence energy is

$$
\mathcal E_{\mathrm{coh}}(Z)
=
\frac{1}{2}
\sum_{e\in\bar E}
a_e\delta_e^{\mathsf T}W_e\delta_e.
$$

This compatibility structure is part of the target family and may be specialized or omitted in a particular prototype.

## 3. Intrinsic geometry

Define positive intrinsic edge length

$$
\ell_e(Z)
=
\frac{\exp(q_e)}{a_e}
\psi_e\bigl(\delta_e(Z)\bigr),
$$

with $\psi_e>0$. A simple choice is

$$
\psi_e(\delta)=\sqrt{\epsilon^2+\|\delta\|^2}.
$$

The intrinsic distance is

$$
d_Z(i,j)
=
\inf_{\gamma:i\rightsquigarrow j}
\sum_{e\in\gamma}\ell_e(Z).
$$

The current $M_F$ prototype implements a scalar specialization of this edge-length construction.

## 4. Collective-feedback mechanism

A collective statistic may be generated by the substrate:

$$
c=\Psi(x,s,q,\theta).
$$

Collective feedback means that $c$ enters one or more transition equations:

$$
\dot Z=F_0(Z)+F_F(Z,c).
$$

The current executable is of this type. In its minimal form,

$$
c(x)=\frac13\sum_i x_i^2.
$$

This mechanism does not require an admissible manifold or projection operator.

## 5. Projected-admissibility mechanism

A projected variant requires explicit equality and inequality constraints:

$$
\Gamma(Z)=0,
\qquad
H(Z)\geq0.
$$

Then

$$
\mathcal M
=
\{Z:\Gamma(Z)=0,\ H(Z)\geq0\}.
$$

A local or unconstrained model proposes velocity $F_{\mathrm{local}}(Z)$. The actual velocity is the admissible component

$$
\dot Z
=
\Pi_{T_Z\mathcal M}F_{\mathrm{local}}(Z).
$$

For smooth equality constraints and a positive-definite state metric $G(Z)$, a candidate expression is

$$
\dot Z
=
F_{\mathrm{local}}
-
G^{-1}J_{\Gamma}^{\mathsf T}
\left(
J_{\Gamma}G^{-1}J_{\Gamma}^{\mathsf T}
\right)^{\dagger}
J_{\Gamma}F_{\mathrm{local}}.
$$

This expression requires rank, regularity, domain, and numerical-preservation analysis. It is not yet implemented in the repository.

## 6. Combined mechanism

A combined model must retain separate feedback and projection terms:

$$
\dot Z
=
F_0(Z)
+
F_F(Z)
+
F_P\bigl(Z,F_0+F_F\bigr).
$$

Removing $F_F$ must recover $M_P$. Removing $F_P$ must recover $M_F$. Removing both must recover $M_0$.

No equivalence

$$
F_F\equiv F_P
$$

is assumed.

## 7. Dynamic topology and metric

A general system may include

$$
\dot x_i=f_i(x_i,x_{\mathcal N_i},s,q,c),
$$

$$
\dot s_e=g_e(s_e,\delta_e,x_i,x_j,c),
$$

$$
\dot q_e=h_e(q_e,\delta_e,x_i,x_j,c),
$$

$$
\dot c=R(c,\Psi(Z)).
$$

Whether $c$ is algebraically derived or dynamically independent must be declared separately for each model.

## 8. Non-collapse barrier

A target variant may define

$$
U_{\mathrm{barrier}}(Z)
=
\sum_e\frac{B_e}{\ell_e(Z)^{p_e}},
\qquad B_e,p_e>0.
$$

Then

$$
\ell_e\to0^+
\quad\Longrightarrow\quad
U_{\mathrm{barrier}}\to+\infty.
$$

The implication from divergent potential to an unreachable finite-energy boundary remains a proof obligation under explicit energy assumptions.

## 9. Equivariance

For an admissible presentation transformation $g$, the target dynamics should satisfy

$$
F(g\cdot Z)=Dg_ZF(Z),
$$

and every invariant observable $Q$ should satisfy

$$
Q(g\cdot Z)=Q(Z).
$$

Equivariance must be proved or tested separately for $M_0$, $M_F$, $M_P$, and $M_{FP}$.

The label “projected geometry” is licensed only after explicit constraints, projection implementation, preservation tests, code-equation parity, and substrate-path identification all pass.

Until then:

$$
\operatorname{PROJECTED\_CLAIM}=\text{UNVERIFIED}.
