# Proof Obligations

Strong claims are blocked until the following obligations are met.

## P1. Positivity of edge lengths

Prove

$$
\ell_e(Z)>0
$$

for every finite admissible state.

## P2. Metric validity

On each connected component, prove that shortest-path distance is nonnegative, symmetric, zero only on the diagonal, and satisfies the triangle inequality.

## P3. Existence and uniqueness

State regularity conditions under which the finite-dimensional initial-value problem has a unique local solution.

## P4. Boundedness or blow-up classification

Determine parameter regimes yielding bounded trajectories, finite-time blow-up, or loss of connectivity.

## P5. Constraint preservation

For projected variants, prove

$$
\frac{d}{dt}\Gamma(Z(t))=0.
$$

## P6. Non-collapse

Under an explicit conserved or bounded energy, show that finite-energy trajectories cannot reach $\ell_e=0$.

## P7. Equivariance

Prove permutation equivariance and invariant observables under node relabeling.

## P8. Identifiability

Show that the effects of local dynamics, dynamic topology, dynamic metric, and collective feedback can be separated by controlled interventions.

## P9. Numerical convergence

Demonstrate that conclusions survive decreasing time step, alternative integrators, and independent reference implementations.

## P10. Falsifiable novelty

Identify at least one prediction that cannot be reproduced by a simpler static graph or unconstrained local model with matched parameter count.
