# ADR 0001: Separate Mathematics from Ontological Ultimacy

## Status

Accepted.

## Decision

The repository will contain a positive mathematical model and a separate apophatic non-reification schema.

The schema will not be represented as a dynamical object, infinite negation operator, final kernel, or hidden first cause.

## Rationale

Turning negation, recursion, coherence, or the graph itself into an ultimate object merely relocates the foundational claim. The project therefore permits ordinary mathematical construction while refusing the inference from internal formal success to absolute ontology.

## Consequences

- Mathematical objects may be fully defined and tested.
- Claims must specify their theory, presentation class, and invariance group.
- No README, paper, or result may identify the model with reality itself.
- Ontological language must be labeled as interpretation or hypothesis.
