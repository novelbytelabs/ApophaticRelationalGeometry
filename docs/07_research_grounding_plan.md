# Research Grounding Plan

## Purpose

This document defines the path from software-verified prototypes to a research-grounded and falsifiable program.

ARG must not move directly from philosophical motivation or software tests to scientific or ontological claims.

The governing order is now:

$$
\boxed{
\text{research established results}
\to
\text{synthesize consensus}
\to
\text{revise formalism}
\to
\text{experiment only on a residual question}.
}
$$

## Evidence levels

Work proceeds through four distinct levels:

1. **Interpretive principle** — the apophatic non-reification schema.
2. **Mathematical hypothesis** — a coherent family of local, feedback, projected, and combined relational dynamics.
3. **Scientific hypothesis** — one or more ARG mechanisms explain or predict selected systems better than simpler alternatives.
4. **Ontological conjecture** — objective reality has a pluralistic-monist organization.

The interpretive principle is formulated. Contract v1.0 supplies software-verified prototypes for the second level. The third and fourth levels remain unverified.

## Research principles

### Consensus before novelty

Every candidate principle must first be translated into established terminology and audited against mathematics, physics, dynamical systems, control, computation, biology, and philosophy as applicable.

A claim already established elsewhere enters ARG as sourced consensus, not as rediscovery.

### Falsification first

Every strong claim must be paired with a result that would weaken or reject it.

### Separate mechanisms before comparison

Collective feedback, tangent projection, and their combination remain separately measurable. Similar behavior must not erase their mechanistic distinction.

### Baselines before novelty

Every extended model must be compared against simpler systems and the closest established frameworks with matched information, parameter budgets, initial conditions, observation access, and tuning effort.

### Recover known limits

A credible framework should reproduce established special cases when mechanisms are removed or frozen.

### Type every scientific claim

A scientific question may require more than a state–structure pair. The declared context may include:

$$
\mathcal S=
(\mathcal X,F,B_{\mathrm{in}},C_{\mathrm{out}},K,\mathcal U,\mathcal W,\Phi,T).
$$

No input, output, constraint, control, disturbance, observable, or horizon may be omitted when the claim depends on it.

### Separate evidence levels

Every statement must be labeled as one of:

- definition;
- assumption;
- implemented;
- unit-tested;
- proof obligation;
- proved under stated assumptions;
- numerically supported;
- empirically supported;
- consensus C3 or C4 within stated scope;
- interpretively disputed C2;
- plausible synthesis C1;
- open or speculative C0;
- philosophical;
- rejected.

### No ontological promotion

Mathematical or empirical success does not by itself establish that the model is absolute reality.
