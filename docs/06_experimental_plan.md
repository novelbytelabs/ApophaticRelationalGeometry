# Experimental Plan

## Status

All four contract-v1.0 models are implemented and unit-tested:

$$
M_0,\qquad M_F,\qquad M_P,\qquad M_{FP}.
$$

Protocol `ARG-P5-COMP-v1` is frozen before trajectory generation. A development pilot is authorized but has not run. Confirmatory execution and comparative scientific claims remain blocked.

## Canonical models

- $M_0$: local/adaptive substrate without collective feedback or projection.
- $M_F$: $M_0$ plus endogenous collective feedback.
- $M_P$: $M_0$ proposal projected onto $\Gamma(Z)=\frac13x^Tx-c_0=0$.
- $M_{FP}$: $F_0+F_F$ followed by node projection, with retained $s/q$ feedback.

At the same regular full state,

$$
\boxed{f_{FP}=f_P},
$$

but this does not imply identical trajectories.

## Primary comparison — $M_F$ versus $M_P$

### Question

Are collective feedback and constant-amplitude projection dynamically distinguishable under the full-state map

$$
O_{\mathrm{full}}=(x,s,q)?
$$

### Frozen metric

For sampled trajectories $A=(a_k)$ and $B=(b_k)$:

$$
D(A,B)
=
\sqrt{
\frac{2\sum_k\|a_k-b_k\|_2^2}
{\sum_k\|a_k\|_2^2+\sum_k\|b_k\|_2^2+10^{-30}}
}.
$$

The positive detection threshold is $0.02$. The scoped observational-equivalence margin is $0.002$. Every positive direction effect must also exceed ten times its numerical floor.

The independent design unit is the initial direction. Five $c_0$ levels are repeated conditions within a direction.

## Secondary comparisons

### $M_0$ versus $M_F$

Tests the effect of endogenous collective feedback against the no-feedback substrate.

### $M_0$ versus $M_P$

Tests the effect of exact constant-amplitude projection against the unprojected substrate.

### $M_P$ versus $M_{FP}$

Tests whether retained $s/q$ feedback produces later node divergence despite the same-state identity $f_{FP}=f_P$.

Secondary results are developmental and cannot independently promote a scientific claim.

## Frozen initial-condition design

Twenty-four deterministic Fibonacci directions on the $z>0$ hemisphere are crossed with

$$
c_0\in\{0.05,0.10,0.25,0.50,1.00\},
$$

with shared $s_0$ and $q_0$. This yields 120 configurations.

A direction-level SHA-256 rule freezes:

- 10 pilot directions, 50 configurations;
- 14 held-out confirmatory directions, 70 configurations.

Confirmatory trajectories may not be generated during the development pilot.

## Numerical policy

Reference integration uses fixed-step RK4 with

$$
T=10,
\qquad
\Delta t=10^{-3},
$$

and observations every $0.01$.

Every decision-bearing run is repeated at

$$
10^{-3},\quad5\times10^{-4},\quad2.5\times10^{-4}.
$$

DOP853 with frozen tolerances provides the alternate-integrator check. Projected models use segmented integration with the same retraction cadence.

The direction-specific numerical floor is the maximum relevant RK4-refinement and RK4-versus-DOP853 discrepancy across its repeated conditions and compared models.

## Interventions and ablations

Frozen controls include:

- feedback removal;
- projection removal;
- removal of both mechanisms;
- exogenous replay of the paired $M_F$ statistic $c(t)$ without fitting;
- frozen $s$;
- frozen $q$;
- frozen $s$ and $q$;
- all six node permutations as invariance tripwires.

A physical graph-incidence intervention and soft-penalty control are excluded from contract/protocol version 1.0. Adding either requires a versioned amendment before confirmatory execution.

## Failure, missing-run, and stop rules

- no imputation;
- all failures and singular runs retained;
- lock, split, identity, constraint, and permutation failures block evaluation;
- more than one incomplete confirmatory direction blocks the primary decision;
- more than 10% pilot numerical failures pause the pilot for diagnosis;
- thresholds and held-out configurations cannot be changed after viewing effects.

## Required outputs

- full, node, adaptive-substrate, and intrinsic-geometry trajectories;
- local proposal, feedback vector, combined proposal, and projection correction;
- constraint, tangency, denominator, and retraction diagnostics;
- solver/refinement discrepancies and numerical floors;
- deterministic configuration and raw-output hashes;
- immutable failure log and archive checksum manifest.
