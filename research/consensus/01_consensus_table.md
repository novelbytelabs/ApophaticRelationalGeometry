## Consensus extraction after two baskets

The entered evidence supports the following minimal statements:

1. **Realized behavior is model-and-state relative.** A transition law defines possible evolution, while a particular initial condition selects a realized trajectory.
2. **Finite-time behavior can be strongly state-dependent.** In non-normal systems, disturbance orientation and mode interference can dominate short-horizon amplification or decay.
3. **Nonlinear outcome depends on global phase-space organization.** Basin membership and basin geometry control return to attractors after large perturbations; local linear stability alone need not determine robustness.
4. **Organization can persist without pointwise state preservation.** Under explicit rate and regularity conditions, an invariant manifold may continue as a nearby diffeomorphic invariant manifold after perturbation.
5. **Macroscopic persistence may be statistical.** Almost-invariant regions retain most of an ensemble over a declared horizon even when individual trajectories move or diverge.
6. **Qualitative dynamics may persist under perturbation.** Structural stability is expressed through a declared equivalence such as topological conjugacy and is conditional rather than universal.
7. **State and relational structure may coevolve.** This is standard in adaptive-network science and does not by itself distinguish ARG.
8. **Changing state spaces require explicit identification machinery.** Compatible pushforward and pullback maps are one established solution for smoothly evolving spaces.
9. **Representation equivalence is not one relation.** Observational, definitional, categorical, dynamical, and interpretive equivalence preserve different content.
10. **Persistence is a family, not a primitive scalar.** The audited literature distinguishes norm retention, basin return, invariant-set continuation, ensemble retention, and qualitative conjugacy.
11. **No universal ARG compatibility scalar has been established.** The evidence presently supports a family of scoped mathematical relations, not one field-independent invariant.

## Persistence type discipline

Until CS1 is complete, project documents should use a typed term rather than bare “persistence” wherever possible:

$$
P_{\mathrm{norm}},\qquad
P_{\mathrm{basin}},\qquad
P_{\mathrm{manifold}},\qquad
P_{\mathrm{retain}},\qquad
P_{\mathrm{structural}}.
$$

These labels do not assert that the measures are commensurable. They prevent accidental movement between distinct scientific claims.

## Prohibited inference from this table

This preliminary synthesis does **not** establish that:

- reality is fundamentally a structure–state pair;
- all persistence is spectral, basin-based, manifold-based, probabilistic, or structural persistence;
- one compatibility measure unifies linear, nonlinear, biological, informational, and philosophical persistence;
- an invariant manifold is an enduring physical object;
- a metastable ensemble has metaphysical identity;
- topological conjugacy establishes physical or ontological equivalence;
- transport is unique;
- adaptive networks prove a relational ontology;
- formal equivalence determines metaphysical identity;
- ARG has no possible contribution after the full audit.
